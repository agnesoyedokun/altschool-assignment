Biography

My name is Agnes Tioluwanimi Oyedokun. I am a First-Class graduate of English (Linguistics) from the University of Ibadan, Nigeria. My academic background has given me a strong interest in language, meaning, discourse, and how people communicate.

I am currently transitioning into Computational Linguistics and Natural Language Processing. I am particularly interested in how technology can be used to develop better language technologies for African and low-resource languages, especially Yoruba.

I have also been developing my technical skills in Information Retrieval and NLP, including working with BM25, dense retrieval, Faiss, and SPLADE using Anserini and Pyserini.

I joined AltSchool because I want to strengthen my software engineering foundation and become more confident building technology solutions. My background is in linguistics, but my interests are increasingly focused on the intersection of language, artificial intelligence, and technology.

The School of Engineering gives me an opportunity to develop practical programming and engineering skills that complement my academic background. I want to understand not only the linguistic side of NLP, but also how the software systems behind these technologies are designed and built.

My Goals

My primary goal is to develop strong software engineering skills that will allow me to build useful and reliable technology.

  • Become proficient in programming and software development.
  • Build a strong foundation in computer science and engineering.
  • Develop practical skills through real-world projects.
  • Strengthen my understanding of systems used in AI and NLP.
  • Build applications that address problems relevant to African languages.
  • Continue developing my skills in Natural Language Processing and Information Retrieval.
  • Contribute to language technologies for underrepresented and low-resource languages.
